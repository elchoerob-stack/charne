How to use Foreman← Back

Foreman does two jobs

It answers and diagnoses — you describe a problem, it works it out with you. And it does the work — you record a job once, and it performs that job itself, in the background, as often as you like.

Ask it something

Describe a fault in the advisor's own words. Foreman ranks the likely causes from its playbooks, checks Evolve/Infomedia/SMS, asks you one question, then gives you numbered steps and how to verify the fix.

Give it a job

Record a booking once with the Recorder. Press Make a task. Now Foreman can do that booking itself — with different data each time, on a schedule, while you are doing something else.

Your first hour

Tap each one as you finish it. Your progress is kept on this device.

1 · Asking it things

Set the dealer code first (top of the screen) — everything is filed under it. Then just type, or press 🎙 to dictate, or 🖼 to attach a photo of the screen.

"Invoice on job card 48812 is not posting to Evolve at Kimberley"
"Customer says she never got the OTP for the brake authorisation"
"How many of these contacts can we actually reach?" (after uploading a contacts export)

Watch the grey activity feed under your question — it shows every check it ran, so you can see why it concluded what it did.

Four modes

ModeUse it when
QuickYou want one fact, fast.
Think defaultA real problem. It gathers evidence before answering.
DeepSomething odd, or outside CMS — browser, tablet, carrier, Windows. Adds web search.
CouncilStubborn. Three specialists each write a brief and Foreman cross-checks them.
The bit that makes it smarter over time: when something is fixed, tell it — "that fixed it". Foreman writes the resolution onto the case, and that becomes a playbook it recognises next time. Skip this and it never learns.

2 · Reports

In the Work tab, under Reports:

  • ＋ Workshop export — a CMS bookings export becomes a dashboard: close rates, advisor and dealer tables, carry-over abuse, weekly trend, and the things to act on.
  • ＋ Marketing contacts — a contacts export becomes a validated SMS and e-mail send list, with the five-sheet workbook and CSVs.

Then press Discuss and ask it things like "which advisor at Ermelo should I coach first?"

These come out in CMS branding, on purpose — they are what you hand to a dealer principal or the CMS team. The app itself is Foreman; the reports are CMS.

3 · Recording a job

  1. Open the CMS screen where the job starts.
  2. Click the Foreman Recorder extension → give it a title → Start.
  3. Do the job exactly as you normally would. If something goes wrong, add a note at that moment.
  4. Stop → Send to Foreman.

The recording is useful three ways: SOP writes it up as a training procedure, Diagnose mines the errors and failed requests it captured, and Make a task turns it into work Foreman can repeat.

Privacy: ID numbers, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and passwords are masked in the browser, before anything is sent. Masked values never end up in a task — you supply them per run.

4 · Turning it into a task

  1. On the recording, press Make a task. Everything you typed becomes a named field you can change per run.
  2. First run only: Connect CMS. A browser opens, you log in once. Only the session cookie is kept — your password is never stored.
  3. Press Run. Tick Practice run the first time to walk the steps without touching CMS, or Watch it happen to see the browser.

The run goes into a background queue. Carry on working; check it under Work, and open the log of any run to see each step and a screenshot if it failed.

Why it does not break every time CMS changes: each step remembers seven ways to find its element — test id, role, label, placeholder, id, visible text, CSS. If they all miss, Claude looks at the page and picks the right one, then writes that repair onto the task so it sticks. If it is not confident, it stops rather than guessing — a wrong click makes a real booking.

5 · The board, schedules and agents

Press Board. Five lanes, and dragging a card does the real thing:

Drop it inWhat happens
To doManual lane. Any schedule switches off.
ScheduledSchedule switches on and the next run is worked out.
PausedKeeps the schedule but stops it firing.
Done / Needs attentionFilled in from the last run.

Dragging works with your thumb on the phone, not just a mouse.

Schedules

⏰ on any card: daily, chosen weekdays, every N minutes, or cron. Times are Centurion wall-clock — "08:00" means 08:00 where you are.

Agents

An agent is a named worker with a job description and the tasks it owns. It runs them in order, then writes you a report: what got done, what did not and why, and what to do next. It appears on the board and in your chat.

Build this one first: an agent called Morning bookings, brief "Loads and confirms the day's service bookings before the workshop opens", owning your booking task, scheduled weekdays at 07:00. You read the result over coffee instead of doing the work.
Scheduled runs are skipped if there is no saved CMS session. If a schedule seems not to have fired, that is the first thing to check — reconnect under Work → Connect CMS.

6 · Your phone

Press Phone for a QR code. Scan it, then use the browser menu → Add to Home screen. Everything works there: dictate the advisor's description, photograph the error, read the steps back on the workshop floor.

Away from the Wi-Fi

  • Tailscale recommended — install on the laptop and the phone, sign both in. The address then works anywhere, nothing is exposed to the internet, nothing to switch on.
  • Cloudflare tunnel — a public link that works on any device. Foreman refuses to open one unless your access token is 20+ characters, because this puts a server that can drive your live CMS session on the open internet. Treat the link like a password.

7 · Monday, ten minutes

  1. Review → promote the good playbooks Foreman learned from resolved cases; archive the one-offs.
  2. Glance at open escalations and anything in Needs attention.
  3. Ask it: "What did we learn this week?"

When something is wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"No Claude API credentials"Add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY to server/.env and restart. Reports, recordings and tasks still work without it.
Phone cannot reach itSame Wi-Fi, or Tailscale. Use the laptop's address, never localhost — on the phone that means the phone.
A task stopped at a login screenThe saved session expired. Work → Connect CMS again.
A scheduled task never ranAlmost always the missing CMS session (above).
Upload says "no booking rows"The sheet has no recognisable Status/Progress column.
Recorder captured nothingReload the CMS tab after installing the extension. It only records the tab you pressed Start in.
