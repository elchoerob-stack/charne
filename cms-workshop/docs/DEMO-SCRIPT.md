# A ten-minute demo that lands

Written for a dealer principal or a service manager. Have three things open
before you start: `index.html` on the laptop, `workshop.html` on a tablet or
a second window, and `customer.html#demo1234` on your phone.

Reset first: **Setup → Data → Reset the demo dealership.**

## 1 · "What needs me this morning?" (1 min)

Sign in as **Thandi Mokoena, PIN 1111**.

Point at *Needs you*. Not a list of everything — a list of what has gone
wrong, in the order it hurts: a failed Evolve post, an approval that has
been sitting with a customer for two hours, parts on backorder, a health
check half done.

> "Your advisor doesn't have to go looking. The screen tells them."

## 2 · Booking in ninety seconds (2 min)

**New booking.** Type a name and a mobile. Note that Next is grey until the
mobile is a real South African number, and the reason appears under the
field rather than in a pop-up.

Paste a VIN — `WVWZZZ1KZAW123456` — and the make and model fill themselves
and the OEM menus become available. Pick the 15 000 km service; the price is
there. Pick a slot; the load bar shows how full the day is. Confirm.

> "Five steps, and it saves on every one. If a walk-in interrupts them
> halfway through, the draft is waiting when they come back."

## 3 · The bay (2 min)

On the tablet: **Johan Pretorius, PIN 4444**. Open the Polo, **Clock on**,
then **Carry on with the health check**.

Do three things in front of them:
- Tap **Needs work** on a tyre — the card goes red.
- On front pads, type `2.1` — it goes red by itself, because that is what
  the franchise's threshold says.
- Add a photo.

> "He isn't filling in a form. He's telling you what he found, and the
> photograph is the proof."

Finish the check. It tells him what is missing before it goes anywhere.

## 4 · Quoting what he found (1 min)

Back on the laptop, open the job and the **eVHC** tab. Each red and amber
item has **Quote it**. Quote the pads: the reason is already written in the
technician's words, add the part, add it for approval.

## 5 · The customer's phone (3 min)

This is the part that sells it. Open `customer.html#demo1234` on your phone
and hand it to them. Code `4417`.

Let them scroll it themselves. Photographs. Why it matters, in plain words.
Prices including VAT. **Yes, go ahead** and **Not this time** on each item
separately, and a running total at the bottom that changes as they choose.

Have them decline one and approve the rest, sign with a finger, and confirm.

Then point back at the laptop. The board has already moved.

> "No app to install, no login, no phone tag. And you have their signature
> against exactly what they agreed to."

## 6 · Where the money went (1 min)

**Reports.** Health-check completion, upsell presented against upsell sold,
conversion by advisor, hours sold against hours clocked.

> "You'll know by Wednesday which advisor isn't presenting the work, not at
> month end."

## If they ask the hard questions

**"What about Evolve?"** — Open a job, **Financial**. The job card posts when
it opens, the invoice when it is finalised, and every attempt is logged with
Evolve's answer. Failed posts are retried after the cause is fixed — never
re-invoiced, because that puts duplicate documents in Evolve.
(**Setup → Integrations → Training switches** will make one fail on demand
if you want to show it.)

**"Our Wi-Fi is bad in the pits."** — Photos are scaled down before they are
stored, and work is kept on the tablet until there is a connection.

**"We don't call it a job card."** — **Setup → Words we use.** Change it and
it changes on every screen, including the technicians' and the customer's.

**"Our people won't all see the same thing."** — **Setup → Roles.** Tick the
screens each role gets. Sign in as the parts person (PIN 7777) and show them
a shorter menu.

**"Can it look like us?"** — **Setup → Branding.** The dealership's mark
goes on the customer link.

## Close

> "It's one system. The booking, the bay, the customer's phone and the
> financial post are the same job card seen from four sides — and nobody has
> to retype anything for that to be true."
