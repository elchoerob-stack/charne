👋 Choose your leagues

Tick the leagues you want to follow. You'll only be nudged to predict for these on your home page — and they're the ones shown in your logs. You can change this anytime from the leagues menu.

🏉 How this works

📅Fixtures lists every match still to come. Tap one and add your score guess before kick-off.
✅Results shows finished matches with the final score and who called it closest.
🏆Pool is the running scoreboard (the log) for the league you're in — points add up across every match all season.
🔀On the League tab, the row of league buttons at the top lets you switch between logs or start a new one (motorsport, U20 rugby, cricket…) — each has its own scoreboard from zero.
🔒Deleting a prediction, setting a final score, or editing someone else's guess needs the family PIN.
💰Set a pot amount on any match and the season's points leader takes it all.
