How to personalise a book

  1. Open the book and tap ✎ Edit.
  2. Tap any text to change it. Highlighted [brackets] still need filling in.
  3. Tap a photo to replace it, for example with a photo of the seller’s home taken at the valuation.
  4. Tap a button, such as WhatsApp or Sign the mandate, to set where it links to.
  5. Tap ⬇ Save client copy. You get one file to WhatsApp or email to the client. Use Print / PDF for a printable version.
