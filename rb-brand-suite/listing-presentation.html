Good to know

Questions sellers ask us

Why a sole mandate?

One agency running one coordinated campaign. Your home isn’t shown at different prices by different agents, and we can invest fully in premium marketing — which attracts better buyers and better offers.

How long will it take?

Well-priced homes in your area currently sell in about [00] days. Once an offer is accepted, transfer usually takes 8–12 weeks.

What if it doesn’t sell?

We review viewings, feedback and online interest with you every week and adjust the strategy together — never without your agreement.

What do I need to do?

Declutter, keep the home show-ready and get your compliance certificates in order — we’ll help arrange them. We handle everything else.

What does it cost me?

Nothing upfront. Our commission is paid from the proceeds on registration of transfer. See your walk-away estimate on the previous page.

Can I stay while it’s listed?

Of course. Every viewing is arranged with you in advance, and we are present at every single one.
