Step by step

How buying a home works

From first viewing to the keys in your hand — usually around three months, start to finish.

  1. Before you lookGet pre-approved

    A bond originator tells you what you can afford — and sellers take your offer more seriously.

  2. Weeks 1–4View homes

    We shortlist homes that match your wish list and arrange viewings around you.

  3. When you find itMake an offer

    You sign an Offer to Purchase (OTP). Once the seller accepts, it’s a binding agreement.

  4. 1–3 weeksBond approval

    Your bank values the property and approves the home loan. Most offers are subject to this.

  5. With the attorneysFICA & documents

    You provide ID, proof of address and income to the transferring attorneys.

  6. 8–12 weeksTransfer

    The attorneys prepare and lodge the documents at the Deeds Office. You pay transfer costs.

  7. Registration dayIt’s yours

    The property is registered in your name — and we hand you the keys.

Be ready to move fast

Your document checklist

📂

Having these ready means your bond application and FICA go through without delays — and your offer looks stronger.

  • Certified copy of your ID (and your spouse’s, if applicable)
  • Proof of address, not older than 3 months
  • Latest 3 months’ payslips
  • Latest 3–6 months’ bank statements
  • Marriage certificate / antenuptial contract, if married
  • Self-employed: 2 years’ financial statements and tax returns
  • Bond pre-approval certificate
  • Proof of deposit funds, if paying a deposit
